Either way, the next steps are the same: **download the installer**, run it, then activate. We'll cover each step below.

Download or Activation Not Working? Fixes
------------------------------------------

Most download and activation problems trace back to a handful of common causes. 

**Download won't start:** Clear your browser cache, or try a different browser. A blocked pop-up window often stops the download silently. 

**"Invalid activation code" error:** Recheck each character. Codes often confuse the letter O with zero, and the letter I with the number 1. Copy and paste instead of typing. 

**App shows no active license after install:** Sign out of My Kaspersky inside the app, then sign back in. This forces the app to recheck your subscription. 

**Code already used on maximum devices:** A certain number of devices can be used in each plan. Before activating a new device, remove Kaspersky from an old or unused device. 

**Installer blocked by another antivirus:** Running two security tools at the same time can cause conflict. Completely uninstall your old antivirus before installing Kaspersky. 

**No internet connection during activation:** Kaspersky needs to connect to its activation server to confirm your code. Check your Wi-Fi and try again. 

**Download link expired or broken:** Re-sign in to my.kaspersky.com and create a new link for download. The old links found in the email may stop working after a certain time. 

**Activation code rejected as "already registered":** This usually means that the code has been used once before, you may have used it in an earlier installation. Before contacting support, sign in to My Kaspersky to confirm license status and see the 'Subscriptions' tab. 

If none of this works, contact Kaspersky support directly through my.kaspersky.com. Avoid third-party "help" sites that show a phone number attached to every line of text. That pattern is a common sign of a fake support page, not real Kaspersky help. 

Important Note for US Users
````````````````````````````

If you're downloading Kaspersky from the United States, there's something you should know first. 

The US Department of Commerce banned new Kaspersky sales starting July 20, 2024. Kaspersky also stopped pushing software updates to US customers after September 29, 2024. 

This means US-based installs no longer receive new virus definitions or security patches. The software still runs, but protection quality drops over time without updates. 

If you're in the US and need active antivirus protection, look at alternatives that still receive full support, such as Bitdefender, Norton, or Microsoft Defender, which comes built into Windows. 

Outside the US, including the EU, UK, and most other countries, Kaspersky remains available for purchase, download, and full updates as of this writing.
